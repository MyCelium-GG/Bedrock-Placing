# BedrockPlacing

Never aim at the edge again. Stand on a block, look straight ahead and
right-click the air: the block is placed on the side of the floor you stand
on, in the direction you face — just like Bedrock placing.
A white outline previews exactly which cell will be filled, and holding
right-click bridges fast without vanilla's sluggish repeat delay.

- Bedrock-style air-click placement with same-layer lock (jumping or falling never strands a block off your floor)
- Server-authoritative, works in multiplayer (vanilla clients fall back to the normal path)
- Config: `enabled`, `sneakDisables`, `requireSupport`, `reach`, `preview`, `repeatTicks`
- Requires **Mycel**: install it alongside this mod

Reload live with `/mycel reload bedrockplacing`.
