# BedrockPlacing (Forge)

Never aim at the edge again. Stand on a block, look ahead and right-click
the air: the floor extends in the direction you face, Bedrock-style, with a
white preview outline and fast bridging while held.

- Server-authoritative placement through vanilla's own path (stairs orient, waterlogging works)
- Config: `enabled`, `sneakDisables`, `requireSupport`, `reach`, `preview`, `repeatTicks`

Requires **Mycel**. Needs Forge 66.0.9+, Minecraft 26.3, Java 25.
